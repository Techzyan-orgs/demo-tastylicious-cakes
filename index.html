Our Story: Whisking Up Joy!

How a pinch of passion and a dash of magic turned into Tastylicious Cakes.

The Baker's Journey

How it all began…

It started in a tiny home kitchen in Behala, where our founder Mitali spent late nights experimenting with flour, sugar, nolen gur and a whole lot of love. Friends asked for “just one more” — then their friends did too. What began as a weekend hobby became a full-blown obsession with making every celebration unforgettable.

Fun fact: our very first cake was a lopsided strawberry delight that still tasted like magic. 🍓
